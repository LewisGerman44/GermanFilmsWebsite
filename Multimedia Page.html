German Films Logo


To create the logo I decided to use Adobe Illustrator, I found that this tool was easy to use and not to complicated to understand. The first thing I did when designing my logo was to decide what shape and theme I was going to go for, after doing some research and looking at some current film logos. I decided that I wanted to go with a vintage look. After I had decided what logo I was going to make I then opened Illustrator and started to create my logo.

The first step I did was to create the main body of the vintage film camera, I did this by using the rectangle tool and inserting a black rectangle onto my blank canvas. The second step was to create the old film tape holders, I did this by using the eplise tool to create two perfect circles to sit on top of the film camera body, I made one big circle to go at the back of the body and one small circle to sit at the front both of these circles I coloured black. After that I then made very small circles that went around the inner edge of the big circle to act as the holes on the film camera, I did this by making the small circles white. As well as this I also put another circle in the other circle, but this circle was half the size of the small circle at the front of the camera body and I also made it white.

The next step after that, was to add the front of the camera, again I did this by inserting another rectangle but this one is a bit smaller and sits at the front of the main rectangle. After that, I decided to add legs to the camera, I did this by using the line tool and creating a tripod stand with three identical lines and then two smaller lines to join the three lines togther. The next step was adding text to the middle of the camera body, I did this by using the text tool and inserting the text over the body. The final step was to add the camera handle, to do this I used the curvature tool and with the mouse I drew a handle and connected it with back of the main rectangle.



Hamilton Movie Poster


To create the poster I used Adobe Photoshop, I found that again this tool was not to complicated and simple to use. The first step with creating my poster was deciding on what the theme was going to be, I went with Formula One, due to my interest in the sport and my love for motorsport. The next step was deciding on what was going to be on the poster, with this I decided to go for the Formula One driver Lewis Hamilton. The reason for this is that I could create an exciting and dramatic poster. After I knew what it was about I started to gather my assests and create my poster.

The first step with creating my poster was to choose the background of the poster, I decided to go with a dark red and organge sky, I chose this beacuse it would help give a dramatic effect to the poster and that the images would blend in well that I had chosen, due to Hamilton's teams being Red and Black. After I had insterted the background, I then placed the main image of the poster, I went with an image of Lewis Hamilton celebrating a win, the first step was to remove the background and then to help give the image an effect I used the gradient tool to help blend the image with the background. After I had added the main image I could then see how much space I had left to add more images. I decided that I did not want to overcrowd the poster so I made the decision to only add a few more images.

The next two images I added was when Lewis Hamilton used to be with Mercedes, I found images that would work with the poster then and removed the background off them. From this I then added them to the poster, one of the images I placed on the right under Lewis Hamilton celebrating and the other one on the left of him celebrating. To help make sure these images didn't stand out to much I used to gradient tool and added a linear gradient to blend them in with the main image and background. After this to make sure it had the Formula One theme, I decided to add one of Lewis Hamilton's Formula One cars. Once I had picked my image, I used the lasso tool to remove the background and to give the car a clean cut and finish. From this I then insterted the image to the poster and once again used the gradient tool to blend in it within the rest of the poster. The final part was to add the title and caption. To give it the Formula One feel I used the official Formula One font and wrote the title and caption in it, whilst adding an effect to the text to help it blend in.



Hamilton Movie Advert


To create my movie advert I used Audacity, I found that this tool had plenty of editing features and was simple to use. The first step with creating my advert was finding the music to use for the background, I decided to go with the Formula One movie theme instrumental, it help gives a dramatic effect and has a nice rhythm. The next step after that was deciding on the sounds to use within the audio file, I went with some Formula One commentary and the sound of a Formula One car. Once I had decided my sounds, I then downloaded my audio files and inserted them within Audacity. After that, I took the Formula One car sound and made sure to blend the noise with the background music, I did this by fading the car sound out at the end and fading it back in with the background music.

The next step was to add the voiceover to the advert, to do this I used an online generator to create a dramatic movie voiceover. Once the voiceover created, I split into four parts and inserted within the main audio file. As well as this, I also spaced out the voiceover across the whole of the file. I then decided to insert Formula One commentary within the gaps I had created from the voiceover, I have done this to blend the voiceover and commentary together and give it a clean sound. To help blend the sound with the background music I made sure to fade the sounds out and back in with the background music. The final thing I did to make it like a real movie advert was by adding a coming to cinemas commentary at the end of the sound file.
